# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js with PostgreSQL, approved by the user for the requested responsive landing page and server-side payment integration.

## Users
People and businesses in Argentina looking for electromecánico workshop services or replacement parts.

## Product Purpose
Present the workshop, its services and parts, and make it easy to ask questions or begin a purchase.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The public site includes service and parts listings, contact details, FAQs, and a contact form. Mercado Pago Checkout Pro handles payments. Payment state changes only after a verified provider webhook. Product prices and payment methods are shown only when configured or confirmed by Mercado Pago. Workshop identity, contact details, photographs, and testimonials must come from the owner; no fictional business data is to be presented as real.

## Brand Commitments
Red and black are the primary colors. Leave a replaceable logo area and image slots for real workshop photography.

## Evidence on Hand
No workshop name, logo, photographs, address, contact details, product catalog, prices, stock, or testimonials have been supplied. Testimonials start empty.

## Product Principles
- Make contact and quote requests easy to find.
- Keep business claims and catalog data owner supplied.
- Let Mercado Pago determine accepted payment methods and terms.
- Keep payment credentials and card data out of the browser and application database.
